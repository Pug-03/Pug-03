### Hey, what's up?

I'm **Nathaporn Saituptim** — a high school student developer and robotics innovator from Thailand. I build end-to-end products that bridge smart software with physical hardware: AI and computer vision, autonomous robots, full-stack web apps, and embedded IoT.

- **Founder & CEO of [DeePer](https://deeper.in.th)** — a free web app of deep conversation cards for couples, friends & family. 1,050+ curated questions, swipe and take turns on one phone.

- Currently building autonomous systems, full-stack web apps, and embedded IoT solutions
